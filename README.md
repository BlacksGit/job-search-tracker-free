# Free job search tracker: what to log and how to follow up

Suggestions only. Adjust for your field and situation.

## Set goals before you apply

Write your target job titles, pay range, location or remote limit, must-have benefits, and how many applications and networking contacts you will do each week. A number you can check beats "apply more".

## What to log for every application

Date, company, job title, where you found it, how you applied, status, and the date you will follow up. Without the follow-up date, applications disappear.

## Follow-up schedule that works

Thank-you note within 24 hours of an interview. If they gave a reply date, wait until that date, then send one short email. If no date was given, follow up after 5 to 7 business days. One follow-up after that, then move on.

## Interview prep sheet

Write down what the company does, why you want the job, two short stories that show your skills, three questions to ask, the pay and schedule you need, and who interviews you. After the interview write down what they asked while you still remember.

## Keep a contact list

Name, company and role, how to reach them, last contact, next step. Many jobs come through people, so a short message every few weeks keeps you in mind.

## Compare offers on more than pay

Pay, hours, commute, health insurance, paid time off, growth, schedule flexibility. Put the offers side by side before you decide.
